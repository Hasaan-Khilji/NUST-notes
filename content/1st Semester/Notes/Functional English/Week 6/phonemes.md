# Phoneme

A phoneme is the smallest unit of sound spoken in a language that can change the meaning of a word. It cannot be broken down into more sounds. 
Example: /p/ and /b/ if determine if the word is pat or bat.

So basically a sound of a single phonetic alphabet from the [[IPA]].
***But beware, those symbols are actually called [[graphemes|Graphemes]].***

Phoneme is the sound that a grapheme represents.

| **Phoneme**                                                             | **Graphene**                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| A singular sound from a language that can change the meaning of a word. | A symbol from the IPA, that represents a phoneme. |
| It is the sound you hear.                                               | It is a symbol used to represent the sound.       |
| The sound of /p/                                                        | /p/                                               |
