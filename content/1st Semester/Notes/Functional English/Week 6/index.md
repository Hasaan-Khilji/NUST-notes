# Functional English Week 6

Before any classes, I had to prepared for it's [[Week 6#Wednesday|quiz]]. Which included [[phonetics and phonology|Phonetics and Phonology]] and a bit of [[phrases|Phrases]], [[clauses|Clauses]] and [[sentence structure|Sentence Structure]].

### First Lecture
[Sentence Structure Slide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1OWVnI3NvrjLafMCFn8dPUcfbnTz1xf_H/edit?usp=drive_link&ouid=116406561646526131309&rtpof=true&sd=true)

In the first class we did [[sentence structure#Advanced Sentence Structure|advanced sentence structure]]. We started by studying [[finite and infinitive#Finite and Non-finite Construction|Finite and Non-finite construction]]. 
Then moved on to studying  [[1st Semester/Notes/Week 6/Functional English/embedding|Embedding]], which is just placing a clause in a sentence to add detail. Then came [[parallelism|Parallelism]], which makes sentences clear by introducing rhythm. 
After that we made it to the spicy part, [[1st Semester/Notes/Week 6/Functional English/clefts and inversion|clefts and inversion]]. Changing the emphasis not using [[1st Semester/Notes/Week 6/Functional English/word stress|stress]], but by actually reshaping the sentence.
[[modifiers|Modifiers]] was something we had already done a bit before, but this time we added on to it by looking at some [[modifiers#Misplaced Modifiers|misplaced modifiers]] and [[modifiers#Dangling Modifiers|dangling modifiers]] and fixing them. Note that these sentences are completely grammatically correct, but they are confusing. 

Then we did some [[1st Semester/Notes/Week 6/Functional English/mixed construction|mixed construction]] and their fixing.

Came after, the [[sentence fragments|sentence fragments]] and [[run-on sentences|run-on sentences]] and how to [[run-on sentences# Fixing Run-Ons|fix run-ons]] and [[sentence fragments#Completing Fragments|completing fragments]].
