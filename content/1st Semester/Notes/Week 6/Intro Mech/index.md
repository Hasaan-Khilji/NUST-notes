### Velocity of Projectile
![[Drawing 2026-10-12 10.14.45.excalidraw]]

$$
s = vt \\\\
v = v cos(\theta) = \frac {\bar x}{t} \\\\
\Delta y = v_i t + \frac {1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
y_f - y_i = v_i t - \frac {1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
-y_i = v_i sin(\theta) t -  \frac{1}{2} g t^2 \\\\\\
$$

At $\theta = 0\degree$,
$$
-y_i = v_i sin (0) - \frac {1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
-y_i = 0 - \frac{1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
-y_i = -\frac{1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
y_i = \frac{1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
t^2 = \frac{2 y_i}{g} \\\\
t = \sqrt{\frac{2 y_i}{g}}
$$
At $\theta = 30\degree$,
$$
-y_i = v_i sin (30) - \frac {1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
\frac{1}{2} g t^2 - v_i sin(30) - y_i = 0
$$

This equation is Quadratic in t, so:

$$
t = \frac {v_i sin (30) \pm \sqrt{v_i^2 sin^2(30) - 2 (g) (-y_i)}} {g}
$$
