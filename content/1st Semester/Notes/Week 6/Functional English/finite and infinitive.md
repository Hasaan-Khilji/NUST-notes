# Finite Verbs

Finite verbs are tense dependent. They change with the change of tense or subject.
First and second form of verbs are always finite.

- #### Example
	- want changes to want/wants depending on the subject. And changes to wanted in case of past tense.
	- Same for every other verb.

# Infinitive Verbs
<br>
Infinitive verbs are independent of tense or subject. These include "to" form of verbs.

- #### Examples
	- to learn
	- to eat
	- to hangout
	- Same for every other verb

## -ing form

-ing form (4th form) of verbs can be finite or infinitive depending on how they are used.
If they are used as a gerund or participle they are infinitive.
But if they're used as the main verb in a continuous tense, they are finite.

- #### Examples
	- Ali is \[writing] a paragraph.
	- Sara will be \[writing] an essay.
	- Shaneel has been \[brainstorming] for 4 hours.<br>
	All of these are main verbs in their respective sentences.
