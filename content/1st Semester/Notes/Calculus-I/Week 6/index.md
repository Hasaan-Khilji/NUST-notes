### Infinite Limits

IN the second half of the second chapter we studied [[1st Semester/Notes/Calculus-I/Week 6/infinite limits|infinite limits]].

Remember when we studied this limit?$$\lim_{x \to 0}f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$$
We said that the limit is undefined because when we approached from the left f(x) approached -ve infinity and as we approached from the right, f(x) approached +ve infinity. OK, fair enough, limits only exist when right and left hand limits are the same.

But what if the right and left hand limits both approach infinity in the same direction?
